What does a kilogram of milk cost you to feed?

Feed is the biggest bill on a dairy or feedlot farm. Write your ration and your own local prices, see the cost in your currency, and decide: keep the ration, or ask a veterinarian to review it.

Write your ration

Pick your country and animals. Enter the amounts you feed per head per day, as bought (fresh weight), and the price you pay. Each animal group is calculated on its own. What you type stays in your browser.

How it works, and what it does not do

  • Your numbers, your currency. We show no reference feed prices for your country yet, because we found no dated, sourced price list we could trust. Prices you type are the only prices used.
  • Economics only. The result tells you what the ration costs and earns. It does not say the ration is nutritionally correct. A ration review by a veterinarian does that.
  • Nothing is sent without you. The review request is text you copy and send yourself.
